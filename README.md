# Makezi

Build the Complete MAKEZI Website and Responsive Web Application based on the attached MASTER PROMPT, MAKEZI Design System final zip archive, Brand Book 3.0 PDF, and Brand Book Review presentation.

Implement the full two-sided marketplace (انتخاب آگاهانه در زیبایی):
1. Brand Tokens & Design System: Reuse the design tokens from MAKEZI Design System (Brand Ink #101C22, Brand Lime #D1F068, Brand Pink #FF70A6, Brand Paper #F7F9F5), Vazirmatn Persian typography, RTL layout, icons, dot patterns, logo SVG, and light/dark theme toggle (default light with dark mode support).
2. Public Marketing Website: Header with logo, navigation, search entry, Persian hero headline, featured salons with evidence-based cards, transparency/trust features (registered prices, verified credentials, booking-verified reviews), model gallery teaser, and comprehensive footer.
3. Salon Discovery & Search: Interactive filters by service category (hair, nail, skin, makeup, etc.), neighborhood/location, verified status badge, price range, and date/time availability.
4. Evidence-based Salon Profile: Salon identity, verified badges, registered price list, staff specialists, verified customer reviews (with dimensional ratings), authentic portfolio, and direct booking trigger.
5. Interactive Booking Flow: Step-by-step service selection, specialist selection, Persian date/time picker, transparent price breakdown, customer details, and booking confirmation modal/summary.
6. Model Inspiration Gallery: Real beauty inspiration tagged by category, service, and creator salon with direct link to book or view salon.
7. Customer Account & Bookings: Active and past appointments management with status badges, rescheduling/cancellation requests, and review submission.
8. Salon Portal & Onboarding: Salon profile onboarding wizard (identity, services, pricing, portfolio upload, credentials) and salon dashboard for managing appointments and reviews.
9. Internal Administration / Verification: Verification queue for pending salons and credentials, marketplace metrics, and content moderation.
10. Respect all open decisions explicitly (documenting defaults and assumptions without inventing unauthorized legal/financial commitments). Ensure all Persian copy, numbers (Farsi digits option), RTL layout, and responsive breakpoints (mobile, tablet, desktop) are pixel-perfect.
